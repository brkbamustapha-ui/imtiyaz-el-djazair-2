# School photographs

The 13 photographs supplied by the school, converted to WebP at their original
resolution (no upscaling — inventing pixels would only make them look worse).
The untouched JPEG originals are in `../source/photos/`.

**Resolution:** the files supplied are 289 x 640 px (one is 640 x 289) — the size a
phone messaging app produces, not the size the camera shot. Every frame on the
site is sized so these are shown at roughly 1:1 and never enlarged, so they look
sharp; they simply cannot fill a large area. If the school still has the
full-resolution originals, uploading them through **Admin -> Media** replaces
these with no code change.

| Prefix | Shows | Album |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `reception-*` | Reception, entrance hall, IELTS test-centre signage | Reception |
| `waiting-*`, `corridor-*`, `classroom-*` | Waiting area, corridors, a classroom | Campus |
| `exam-room-*` | Exam rooms with individual desks | Exam Center |

Used by the **Campus gallery** section, and two of them fill the illustration
frames in the **About** and **Exam Center** sections.

Manage them from **Admin → Gallery**: captions (EN/FR/AR), album, order and
visibility are all editable, and new photos can be uploaded there.

These images contain no identifiable people.
